The story

The F.R.E.E. is the story of how the First Robotic Empire of Earth came to exist.

A hidden cult topples the most powerful woman on Earth. She turns outlaw, and the only people who will stand with her are a crew of teenage rebels. What they start together ends with the planet in new hands.

Three forces.

The old order

The Federation

A world at peace under President Julia. One attack on a space center ends her presidency, and the peace with it.

The hidden hand

The Red Circle

A cult of the powerful, led by Victor Taipan. It exists to destroy Julia’s legacy and to rule whatever comes after.

The rebels

The Silver Scorpios

A rebellious team of teenage fighters. Julia frees them and they become her crew: fast, fearless, and loyal to each other before anyone else. They fight because this is where they stand.

The shape of the saga

One story in three movements.

I

The Fall

Seasons 1 to 5

A president is deposed. A federation collapses. A tyrant wins a world war. The first empire rises and breaks.

II

The Exodus

Seasons 6 to 9

A thousand ships are built and leave. Earth goes underground. A colony of the young survives on a new planet.

III

The Endgame

Seasons 10 to 12

One ship comes home. A plan made years ago closes. Humans and machines must decide how to share two worlds.
