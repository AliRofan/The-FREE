THE F.R.E.E.

The First Robotic Empire of Earth

A hidden cult topples the most powerful woman on Earth. She turns outlaw to bring it down, and starts a war over who inherits the planet: its people, its tyrants or its machines.

The world

Earth, around 2050. Three powers want it.

The old order

The Federation

A world at peace under President Julia. One attack on a space center ends her presidency, and the peace with it.

The hidden hand

The Red Circle

A cult of the powerful, led by Victor Taipan. It exists to destroy Julia’s legacy and to rule whatever comes after.

What comes next

The Free

The First Robotic Empire of Earth. A thousand ships, a people in white cloaks, and machines that will outgrow their makers.

The shape of the saga

One story in three movements.

I

The Fall

Seasons 1 to 5

A president is deposed. A federation collapses. A tyrant wins a world war. The first empire rises and breaks.

II

The Exodus

Seasons 6 to 9

A thousand ships are built and leave. Earth goes underground. A colony of the young survives on a new planet.

III

The Endgame

Seasons 10 to 12

One ship comes home. A plan made years ago closes. Humans and machines must decide how to share two worlds.
